What's Not Working

The issues behind the 3.2 score

📚

Every Caption Follows the Same Dead Format

All 21 posts this month follow an identical mechanical template: one or two announcement lines, then a square-bracket keyword dump like [Solar Yaan, RenewX 2026, Chennai Trade Centre, Solar Power...], then 5 hashtags. Those bracket blocks are SEO meta-tags that were never removed before publishing. Every professional reading the post knows immediately that a tool generated this and nobody reviewed it. There are no hooks, no storytelling, no CTAs. The audience is handed information and then abandoned.

The fix: every caption needs this structure
1
Hook — one bold line that earns the click to "see more"
2
Context — 2 lines of what's happening and why it matters
3
Insight — what the reader takes away from this
4
CTA — one direct ask: comment, DM, follow, share
5
3-5 Hashtags — no keyword dumps, no square brackets
🎨

No Visual Brand System

Six distinct font styles appear across 21 posts. Backgrounds go from sky blue to pitch black to red sandstone to white. Logo placement shifts corner to corner. The Earth Day post uses a cursive script seen nowhere else. A viewer cannot identify a SolarYaan post in 1 second because nothing is consistent enough to hold onto.

Fix

Lock in 2 colors (orange #F5820A + navy #0A1E3D), 1 headline font, 1 body font, logo always bottom-right. Build 5 templates and never deviate.

🔗

Page Setup Fails the First Impression

The company description is two vague sentences. The tagline "Lets Connect With Nature!!!" has a grammar error and sounds like a lifestyle app, not a B2B inverter manufacturer. The website points to howd.in, which is unrelated to SolarYaan. Any serious procurement decision-maker landing here will leave without reaching out.

Fix

Rewrite About with products, geography, USP, and a follow CTA. Fix website URL. Fix the tagline apostrophe. Today.

📷

57% of Posts Were About One Expo

In a single month, 13 out of 21 posts were about RenewX — countdowns, Day 1, Day 2, recaps, highlight reels. Every post used the same structure. By Day 3 the audience had tuned out completely. The categories that drive real engagement — customer stories, CEO voice, education — had near-zero representation.

Fix

Max 3 posts per event. Apply the 7-pillar content calendar going forward (see Action Plan).

📋

The IPO Got 12 Likes

SolarYaan is heading for an IPO. That is a massive brand moment. The post announcing it got 12 likes because it was written as a detached observer commenting on their own news: "It's inspiring to see a Gujarat-based brand scaling up..." — written as if they were watching someone else's IPO. No founder voice. No emotion. No story.

Fix

Write a first-person post from the founder. Tell the real story: why they started, what 5 years of building looks like, what the IPO means for the team. That post will do 200+ likes easily.

👤

Brand Name Has 3 Spellings

"SolarYaan", "Solar Yaan", and "Solar yaan" all appear across posts published in the same month. This is a basic editorial error that signals no review process is in place before publishing.

Fix

Decide on "SolarYaan" and add it to every post template. One brand name. No exceptions.
