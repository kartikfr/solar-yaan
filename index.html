Part 1: What SolarYaan Is Doing Wrong

Six issues behind the 3.2 score

📚

Every Caption Follows the Same Dead Format

Every post ends with a square-bracket keyword dump like [Solar Yaan, RenewX 2026, Chennai Trade Centre, Solar Power...] followed by 5 hashtags. Those bracket blocks are SEO meta-tags that were never removed before publishing. No hooks, no storytelling, no CTAs anywhere.

Every caption needs this structure
1
Hook: one bold line that earns the click to "see more"
2
Context: 2 lines of what's happening and why it matters
3
Insight: what the reader takes away
4
CTA: one direct ask: comment, DM, follow, share
5
3-5 Hashtags: no keyword dumps, no square brackets
🎨

No Visual Brand System

Six distinct font styles across 21 posts. Backgrounds shift from sky blue to pitch black to red sandstone. Logo placement changes every post. A viewer cannot identify a SolarYaan post in 1 second.

Fix

Lock in 2 colors, 1 headline font, 1 body font, logo always bottom-right. Build 5 templates and never deviate.

🔗

Page Setup Fails the First Impression

The company description is two vague sentences. The tagline "Lets Connect With Nature!!!" has a grammar error and sounds like a lifestyle app. The website URL points to howd.in, which is unrelated to SolarYaan.

Fix

Rewrite About with products, geography, USP, and a follow CTA. Fix the website URL and tagline apostrophe.

📷

57% of Posts Were About One Expo

13 of 21 posts in a single month were about RenewX: countdowns, Day 1, Day 2, recaps, all same structure. Customer stories, CEO voice, and education had near-zero representation.

Fix

Max 3 posts per event. Apply the 7-pillar content calendar going forward.

📋

The IPO Got 12 Likes

The IPO announcement post got 12 likes. It was written as a detached observer: "It's inspiring to see a Gujarat-based brand scaling up..." No founder voice. No emotion. No story.

Fix

Write a first-person founder post. Why they started, what 5 years looks like, what the IPO means. That post will do 200+ likes.

👤

Brand Name Has 3 Spellings

"SolarYaan", "Solar Yaan", and "Solar yaan" all appear in the same month. No review process before publishing.

Fix

Decide on "SolarYaan" and add it to every post template. One brand name. No exceptions.
